## Foundations and future direction

RIF asks whether nested bodily rhythms and cortical connectome modes exhibit phase relationships associated with conscious access. Its current software consists of toy utilities and synthetic examples. The revised paper defines the quantities, discloses a weighted one-dimensional input defect found by static inspection, and proposes tests that could reject the hypothesis. No participant result or clinical conclusion is established.
